## Quantity Items

These are parts you may or may not have on hand. Ordering “one unit” of these items is often enough to make dozens of PLatform kits.

- [Female headers for jumpering from the microcontroller](https://www.adafruit.com/product/598)
- Short LED strip. A strip with spacing of 60 LEDs per meter, cut to 10 LEDs fits nicely on the board. This is short enough that USB power can power it, but long enough to be interesting. You often see these marketed as [Neopixel](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2541), but can be found under manufacturer part numbers like `WS2812B` or `SKC6812`.
- Hardware:
    - M3 screws & nuts, 10mm, 16mm
        - If using the optional laminated pull-out reference card, you want 16mm M3 with low-profile heads.
    - M2 screws & nuts, 20mm
    - [M3 lock nuts](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CX16JZ6Z?th=1)
    - [M3-compatible rubber feet](https://www.mcmaster.com/9540K51/)
    - [M3 standoffs (40mm)](https://www.mcmaster.com/95947A069/)
    - [Nylon washers](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BNB1KVCW?th=1) and [10mm standoffs](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0913DCK23?th=1)
- 1/8" or 3mm Acrylic sheet for the laser cutter. I used scrap I had on hand.

## Commodity Items

- Wire : It's helpful to have at least three colors to represent power, ground, and data.
- Solder
- Heat-shrink tubing
- Breadboard jumper wires
- USB-C cable for programming/powering
- Glue for securing the LED strip. (I used a hot glue gun. E6000 didn't adhere well.)
- Printer and laminator for the pull-out reference card

## Tools

- Soldering iron
- Multimeter
- Hot glue gun
- Small needle-nose pliers to hold a lock nut while tightening
- Small hex screwdriver or allen wrench to tighten the M3 screws
- Tweezers are helpful
- Rotary etching tool, such as a Dremel with etch bit, if you plan on making several and want to etch in serial numbers.
